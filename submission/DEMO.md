Status: recording not made or hosted. This walkthrough is not a demo recording.

thread. Use a clean scratch copy of review-fixtures; do not record real documents,
credentials, unrelated chats, or desktop notifications. Do not use the standalone
preview as proof of real chat handoff: its chat sending is mocked.

   host sampling or CLI setup. Do not disclose account credentials on screen.
